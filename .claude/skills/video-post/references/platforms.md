# Platform rules

Last verified: 2026-10-31. Re-check anything marked ⚠ (sources conflict) in the platform's own app or help centre before relying on it, and refresh this file if it's older than ~6 months.

## YouTube long-form (16:9)
- Title: 100 characters max; the first ~60 show in search/suggested, so front-load them.
- Description: 5,000 characters max; the first ~2 lines show above "more".
- Tags: 500 characters total.
- Chapters: timestamps in the description, the first at 0:00, at least 3, each at least 10 s.
- Thumbnail: 1280×720 (16:9), JPG/PNG. Size limit ⚠: 2 MB was the long-standing limit; newer sources report 50 MB on desktop ([subsub.io](https://learn.subsub.io/thumbnail-youtube)). Stay under 2 MB to be safe.
- Captions: upload `.srt` in Studio → Subtitles.
- Analytics to collect in the retro: Intro (% still watching at 30 s — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415)), average view duration, CTR.

## YouTube Shorts (9:16)
- Max length: 3 minutes (since October 2024) — [CapCut summary](https://www.capcut.com/resource/max-length-of-youtube-shorts), [Shortsync](https://shortsync.app/resources/youtube-shorts-upload-requirements-2026).
- Vertical 1080×1920.
- Custom thumbnails for Shorts ⚠: availability and size differ by account and are still rolling out ([Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/social-media-marketing/youtube-shorts-custom-thumbnails-no-ab-testing/)). Pick a strong cover frame either way.
- Use "Related video" to link the long-form.

## TikTok (9:16)
- Caption: 2,200 characters in most sources ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/tiktok-video-descriptions-now-have-a-2200-character-limit-388204)); ⚠ one source reports 4,000 at upload.
- Max length ⚠: varies by account and upload method; check the app.
- Keep captions and key text out of the bottom ~20% and the right-hand action column.

## Instagram Reels (9:16)
- Max length ⚠: 3 minutes since January 2025 ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-length)); some later sources report up to 20 minutes, rolling out by account ([inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-can-now-be-20-minutes-long-new-time-limit-explained-2025)).
- Caption: 2,200 characters.
- Hashtags ⚠: the long-standing limit was 30; some 2025–26 sources report a much lower cap. Use 3–5 specific tags, which is within every reported limit.
- Cover: pick a frame or upload a 1080×1920 cover; the grid crops it to roughly 3:4 around the centre.

## Cross-platform
- Burned-in captions: 69% of people watch video with the sound off in public places; 80% say they're more likely to finish a video with captions (Verizon Media/Publicis, 2019, n=5,616 US adults — [3Play Media](https://www.3playmedia.com/blog/verizon-media-and-publicis-media-find-viewers-want-captions/)).
- Don't upload a TikTok-watermarked file to other platforms; export clean renders for each.
