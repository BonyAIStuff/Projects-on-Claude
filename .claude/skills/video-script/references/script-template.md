## SCRIPT.md

```markdown

Angle: <chosen angle> · Target: <mm:ss> · Words: <n> · Style: STYLE.md v<x.y>

[NEWS N1: <publication>, "<headline>", highlight: "<line>"] [SFX: whoosh + shutter]

## 2 · The question (0:05–0:12)
**SAY:** "<specific question>"
[PUNCH-IN] [SFX: soft hit]

## 3 · Why it matters to you (0:12–0:25)
**SAY:** "…"
[ANIM: <what>] 

## 4 · Chapter 1 — <title> (0:25–2:10)
[CHAPTER CARD: 01 · <title>]
**Analogy — SAY:** "…"
[ANIM: <scene, what moves, what's labelled>]

[TEXT CARD: "<the objection as a question>"]

[REEL 1: 0:25–1:15 · hook card: "<2-second text>"]

## 4 · Chapter 2 — … (repeat)

## 5 · Impact on you (~7:30–8:45)
**SAY:** "…"
[B-ROLL: <stock search terms>] [ANIM: before/after]

## Summary
- Runtime estimate: <mm:ss> (<words> words at 150 wpm)
- Chapters: 0:00 <…> · 0:25 <…> · …

```

## Cue notation
| Cue | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[NEWS N#: …]` | Real article screenshot, spotlight + push-in, the quoted lines highlighted one by one |
| `[ANIM: …]` | Motion-graphic scene in STYLE colours (analogy, mechanism, before/after) |
| `[B-ROLL: …]` | Real or stock footage; give search terms. Licensed stock only. |
| `[STAT: …]` | Big-number card, count-up, source line underneath |
| `[ANNOTATE: …]` | Red-pen circle, underline or arrow on a number or phrase |
| `[SLIDE: …]` | Clean diagram or list, built as animation |
| `[TEXT CARD: …]` | Full-screen line of text (objection, key phrase) |
| `[CHAPTER CARD: …]` | Numbered chapter title |
| `[PUNCH-IN]` | Cut closer on the talking head |
| `[SFX: …]` | Sound from the STYLE palette (whoosh, shutter, ping, pop, typing, impact) |
| `[POLL: …]` | The A/B poll CTA card |
| `[REEL n: in–out · hook]` | A moment to cut as a Reel later |
