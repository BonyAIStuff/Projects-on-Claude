# The channel's video structure (YouTube, 8–12 min)

Agreed with the creator on 2026-10-31. Times are for a ~10-minute video; scale the middle, never the opening.

| # | Segment | Time | What it does | Visual treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold open: the result** | 0:00–0:05 | Show the outcome the video explains, with the creator's voice and on-screen text from the first frame | `[NEWS]` animated headline (spotlight + push-in), or licensed stock B-roll of the outcome. No TV news clips unless licensed. If neither exists, the creator's punchiest line to camera with a big text card. |
| 2 | **The intriguing question** | 0:05–0:12 | One specific question the video promises to answer | Punch-in on the creator; caption with highlighter on the key word; soft `[SFX]` hit |
| 3 | **Why it matters to you** | 0:12–0:25 | One or two lines: what this means for the viewer's money, job or business, plus what they'll understand by the end | `[ANIM]` simple icon or `[STAT]` teaser |
| 4 | **The concept, in 3–4 chapters** | 0:25–~7:30 | Each chapter (≈ 1.5–2 min): **analogy → how it really works → one stat as proof → one viewer objection answered**. Between chapters: a **fun-fact re-hook** ("Here's the crazy part…") | Analogy as `[ANIM]` scene; mechanism as `[SLIDE]`/`[ANIM]` diagram; stat as `[STAT]` card with source and `[ANNOTATE]` circle; objection as on-screen question card; chapter card at each start |
| 5 | **Impact on you** | ~7:30–8:45 | The deeper "so what": how this strategy shows up in what the viewer pays, earns or decides | `[B-ROLL]` everyday situations; `[ANIM]` before/after |
| 6 | **Spot it yourself + the debatable question** | ~8:45–10:00 | 2–3 other places this strategy hides ("Next time you order on…"), then a question people will argue about, with the A/B poll | `[B-ROLL]`/logos montage; `[POLL]` card with red-pen arrow to "Vote A or B in the comments" |

## Rules that make it work

- **First 5 seconds**: result + voice + on-screen text together. Footage alone, a logo sting or "Hi guys" loses the viewer.
- **The question must be specific and answerable**: "Why would PhonePe *want* to start charging you?" beats "Ever wondered how UPI works?". Pay it off by the end of chapter 2 at the latest, and open a new question as you do.
- **Something new on screen at least every 10 seconds** (cue, punch-in, B-roll, graphic). See `/format-check`.
- **Stats live inside chapters**, one per point, never a list of numbers.
- **Objections sound like the viewer**: "Now you might be thinking…", "But wait, doesn't that mean…".
- **Re-hook every 60–90 seconds** with a fun fact, a surprising turn or a question.
- **No outro padding**: no "that's it for today". End on the poll question.

## Reels (cut later from the finished YouTube video)
Each `[REEL n]` tag marks 30–60 seconds that work alone. Best sources: the cold open + question (re-hooked), a chapter's analogy, a striking stat with its impact, a fun fact, the debatable question. Each gets a 2-second hook card and ends on a point or a question, plus "full breakdown on YouTube".
