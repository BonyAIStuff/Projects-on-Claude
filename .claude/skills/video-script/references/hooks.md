# Hooks, questions and closers

A hook is two parts said back to back in the first ~10 seconds: the **result** (what happened) and the **question** (the gap the video closes). The gap must feel specific and closeable: viewers get curious about things they half-know, not things that sound like homework.

## Cold-open line formulas (the result)
| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| **Headline + twist** | "UPI just got a price tag, and the people charging it aren't who you think." |
| **Number that shouldn't be true** | "<Company> earns more from a <small fee> than from <the thing you think it sells>." (only with a sourced number) |
| **Before/after** | "In <year> this cost you nothing. Now someone pays <x>%." |
| **"You already did it"** | "If you ordered food this week, you already paid for this strategy." |
| **Contrarian** | "Free UPI was never free." |

## Intriguing-question formulas
| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| **Why would they…?** | "So why would a company *want* to make you pay?" |
| **Who really pays?** | "But who's actually paying this fee, the shop or you?" |
| **What happens next?** | "And what happens to your ₹10 chai when it kicks in?" |
| **How is that possible?** | "How does a company lose money on every order and still grow?" |

Avoid: "Ever wondered…?", "In this video I'll explain…", "Let's dive in", greetings before the hook, and questions answerable with yes/no.

## Re-hook lines (between chapters)
"Here's the crazy part…" · "But that's not even the smart bit." · "Now it gets interesting." · "This is where most people get it wrong." Use each at most once per video.

## Debatable closers (with A/B poll)
Good closers split a smart audience roughly in half and have no obviously right answer.
| Formula | Example | A / B |
|---|---|---|
| **Fair or not?** | "Is a 0.4% fee on big merchants fair?" | Long overdue / A tax on digital India |
| **Who wins?** | "Who wins this, the platforms or the shops?" | Platforms / Shops |
| **Would you?** | "Would you pay ₹5 extra to skip the queue?" | Yes, instantly / Never |
| **Genius or greedy?** | "Is this pricing genius or just greedy?" | Genius / Greedy |

Avoid closers with a moral right answer, or that invite attacks on a person.
