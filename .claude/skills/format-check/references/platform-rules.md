# Platform rules

Set by the creator on 2026-10-31. Thresholds marked (M) are measured by `scripts/measure.py`; the rest are checked in the composition and the contact sheet.

## YouTube long-form

**Format**
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. (M)

**Everything inside the frame**
- Logos, graphics, phone mock-ups, article cards: inside the 5% action-safe box (cyan) for their whole time on screen, including during push-ins and whips once they've landed.
- All text (captions, labels, CTAs, source credits): inside the 10% title-safe box (yellow).
- Nothing important clipped by the frame edge at any moment. A push-in must stop before the masthead/headline leaves the frame.

**Smooth transitions**
- Every change between different scenes (talking head ↔ B-roll ↔ article ↔ graphic) uses the STYLE transition: a whip (0.25–0.45 s, skew settling to 0) with a whoosh. No raw hard cuts between scenes. (M flags one-frame cuts for review)
- Hard punch-ins on the same talking-head shot are allowed; that's an angle change, not a scene change.

**Varied angles for long viewing**
- No more than 10 s without a visual change: a punch-in (+10–15%), a reframe, B-roll, an article or a graphic. (M)
- Alternate punch-in levels (wide 1.0–1.05 ↔ close 1.12–1.17) so consecutive talking-head stretches don't look identical.

**Comfortable sound for long viewing**
- Integrated loudness −14 LUFS (±1), true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. (M)
- Voice always on top; music ≤ 0.3 volume under speech and ≤ 0.6 without speech.
- SFX quieter and sparser than Reels: volume ≤ 0.45, on visible actions only, roughly ≤ 6 per minute of talking head (whips and annotations count; caption clicks only on the first caption of a section).
- No sudden loud moments: impacts ≤ 0.25 volume.

## Instagram Reels

**Format**
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. (M)
- The creator chose full-screen 9:16 Reels (the feed shows a 4:5 crop of it), so keep faces and key text in the centre 4:5 area too.

**Reels safe zone** (red box)
- Keep text, faces' eyes, logos and CTAs out of: top 12% (status bar, "Reels" header), bottom 22% (username, caption, audio line), right 13% (like/comment/share column), left 5%.

**Pace: a cut at least every 3 seconds**
- No more than 3 s without a visual change: hard cut, punch-in, B-roll, article, graphic or a big text card. (M)
- Hard cuts are fine here; whips still welcome.

**Text: bigger and flashier, same brand ("same style, turned up")**
- Captions ≥ 96 px Archivo 800 (YouTube uses 78), 2–4 words per line, centred in the middle third (not at the bottom, where Reels UI sits).
- At least one highlighter-swept word in every caption line (YouTube: 1–2 per line at most).
- Faster pop-in: 0.12 s with a slight scale (0.92→1). No bounce, shake or glitch: those are off-brand.
- Same palette and red rule as STYLE.md: the highlighter stays newsprint yellow; red only as hand-drawn ink.
- SFX can be denser than YouTube (a soft whoosh or pop on most cuts), volume ≤ 0.45.

**Sound**
- −14 LUFS (±1), true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. (M)
