# Projects on Claude

## video-channel/ — business & marketing explainer videos
All video work lives in `video-channel/` (a HyperFrames project). Before any video task, read
`video-channel/CLAUDE.md` and `video-channel/STYLE.md`, and run commands from inside `video-channel/`.

For a new video ("New video: <topic>…"), follow the channel skills in order:
`/video-plan` → `/video-edit` → `/format-check` → `/video-post`. They stop for approval at each gate and track progress
in `video-channel/videos/<slug>/STATUS.md`.
